## Human checks
HOW TO VALIDATE - 4 checks on 1 page. Log in as owner on this slot's app (port 4201). Open a menu from `/menu-intelligence` (the list) so the address becomes `/menu-intelligence/<id>`; or create one with the new-menu button.

### 1. Kg preparation shows 110, not 110.00000000000001
- PAGE `/menu-intelligence/<id>` (any menu you can edit)
- SETUP as owner: in the menu set the guest count to 100, and pick an event type and serving type whose coefficients multiply to 1.1 (if none, type 1.1 into the coefficient box of the dish in the details strip). Make sure a preparation exists in `/recipe-builder` whose yield unit is kg (e.g. a sauce, yield 5 kg).
- DO 1: add that kg preparation as an item of the menu and press its ⓘ icon to open the details strip.
- DO 2: set its portions per diner to 1 and read the field «מנות לאירוע».
- DO 3: add a normal dish with the same coefficient and read its «מנות לאירוע».
- SEE ✓ both fields show `110` exactly, with no long decimals.
- FAIL ✗ any field shows `110.00000000000001` or other digits after the decimal point beyond two.

### 2. Long dish name, info icon and price fit one phone row
- PAGE `/menu-intelligence/<id>` at phone width (browser window 390 px wide, or the device toolbar)
- SETUP: a menu with a dish whose name is long (more than 30 characters, e.g. rename a recipe) and whose price is ₪0.
- DO 1: look at the dish row. DO 2: tap the ⓘ icon next to the name.
- SEE ✓ the name is on one line and ends with «…»; the ⓘ icon sits right next to it, the ₪ price and the trash icon are on the far side of the same row, nothing covers the name; tapping ⓘ opens the dish details.
- FAIL ✗ the ₪0 price sits on top of the name, the ⓘ icon is cut off or cannot be tapped, or the name wraps onto several lines.

### 3. «חלקי» follows the cost and says why
- PAGE `/menu-intelligence/<id>` (open the pricing bar at the bottom with the arrow if it is closed)
- SETUP as owner: a menu with guests, a price per guest typed, and a dish that uses a product with no price (the total cost then shows «חלקי» / a missing-products chip).
- DO 1: read the bar: look at «סה"כ הכנסות», «רווח לאורח», «רווח %» and «מחיר מינימום לאורח». DO 2: price the missing product from the chip, then read the bar again.
- SEE ✓ «חלקי» is never next to «סה"כ הכנסות». After pricing: if «חלקי» is gone from the total cost it is also gone from profit per guest, profit % and minimum price; or, if it stays, a small line under the numbers starts with «למה החישוב חלקי:» and names each reason (e.g. «חסרה תפוקה במתכון: <name>»). Write down the reason line text if it appears.
- FAIL ✗ «חלקי» stays on the profit numbers with no reason line while the total cost has none, or «חלקי» shows next to revenue.

### 4. Revenue with no price at all
- PAGE `/menu-intelligence/<id>`
- SETUP: a menu with guests (e.g. 50), every dish price at ₪0 and the price per guest field empty.
- DO: read «סה"כ הכנסות» in the pricing bar.
- SEE ✓ revenue shows `₪0.00` with the tag «לא הוגדר מחיר ללקוח» next to it.
- FAIL ✗ revenue shows `₪0.00` with no tag, or with the tag «חלקי».
