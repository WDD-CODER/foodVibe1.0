## Decisions
- Sheet heading: `labelledBy` + an own `<h3>` in a `.sheet-head` row with the X, instead of `titleKey` · puts the close button on the title line (the card has no positioned corner); same text «תמחור האירוע», same dialog name.
- One body at a time: the bar's `#meterBody` is not rendered while the sheet is open · two inputs on one `FormControl` do not echo each other's typing (view → model uses `emitModelToViewChange: false`), the plan's "stay in step" was wrong; re-creating the bar on close shows the current values (spec covers it).
- `sell_price` also hidden from staff in the detail fields (`MONEY_DISH_FIELDS` = `sell_price`, `food_cost_money`, `food_cost_pct`) · it is a `DishFieldKey` and shows the ₪ price when the serving type lists it.
- Chip field in the sheet: `align-items: flex-start` · the full-width column stretched the chip across the sheet.
- Page bottom room not changed · «+ הוסף קורס» stays fully above the bar at 1440/1024 (flush, 0 px gap) and at 800; raising `6rem` in `_layout.scss` would also add ~7.5rem of empty room on phones where the bar is gone. For plan 494 (769–1030 px range): bar is 178 px at 1024 and 280 px at 800 with the target boxes shown.
- Unrelated, seen while testing (not fixed, pricing maths out of scope): with a target of 100 the minimum price shows «₪-∞» on desktop and «₪NaN» in the sheet.
- Hero FAB keeps its `.above-bar` lift for staff on desktop (plan Out of Scope).
