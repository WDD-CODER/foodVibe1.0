## Decisions
- "No supplier" card name comes from an optional translator argument of `buildSupplierOrders` (default: the key) · the contract signature has no `t`, and `.ts` may not hold the Hebrew literal.
- A line whose engine `supplierId` points to a supplier that no longer exists goes to the "ללא ספק" card · no name or phone to send to.
- Phone choice: first of `phone` / `phone2` that is a valid WhatsApp mobile, else the first non-empty one · plan P1 only says "use phone2 when phone is empty"; this also rescues a landline in `phone` with a mobile in `phone2`.
- Card total is the plain sum of known line costs (not rounded) · the card totals must add up to `eventFoodCost`; display rounds via `formatMoney`.
- `describeOrderLine` is exported and used by both the message and the cards · one text for qty / unit / purchase, so screen and message cannot differ.
- Short unit: `unit` -> `unit_short_unit`, anything else -> its dictionary key (`kg`, `gram`, `liter`, `ml`), unknown labels (e.g. ארגז) stay as stored · per the plan Decision.
- Business name is `UserService.user_()?.name` · `business.service.ts` does not exist on `origin/mvp` yet (plan 426 not merged).
- Invalid phone shows the warning toast (not the success one) after copying · the text says the number is wrong.
- A cancelled share sheet (AbortError) does nothing; any other share error falls back to copy · closing the sheet is not a failure.
- The new "הזמנות לספקים" button sits in its own group just above the print group of the export sheet · keeps "print" the last action.
- The page opens the sheet through a small `openSupplierOrders()` method (used by the pill, the FAB and the sheet output) · three call sites; the signal is `supplierOrdersEvent_` as the plan names it.
- The component spec registers `MessageCircle` on top of `TEST_LUCIDE_ICONS` locally · `src/testing/test-lucide-icons.ts` is outside the plan scope.
- Dictionary key `supplier_orders_open` is added as the plan lists it, but no template uses it · it is in the plan key list; harmless.
- The toolbar button reuses the existing `.pill` style from `_toolbar.scss` with an icon · no new style.
