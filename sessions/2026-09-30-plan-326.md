## Context

Replaces the two-slot parallel-worktree system (`scripts/session-startup.sh` +
`scripts/claim-parallel-slot.sh`) with a Planner-Worker model: the Planner (main folder, on
`main`) commits plans and `.claude/todo.md` directly to `main`; 3 permanent `wt-N` slots on
fixed ports are claimed via "execute plan NNN"; a Worker may write only inside its plan's
declared Read-Write Scope, enforced live by a PreToolUse hook and, for Cursor (no hooks),
by the `/ship` gate. Full rationale: `docs/brain/decisions/0009-planner-worker-worktrees.md`.
